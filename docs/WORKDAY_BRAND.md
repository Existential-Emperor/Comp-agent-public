## Logo
- Orange arc above "workday" wordmark
- Logo color: Orange ~#F27D35

## Typography
- Primary font: **Calibri** (or system sans-serif)
- Title slides: ~44pt bold, white on gradient bg
- Slide titles: ~28-36pt bold, Ink (#0F2E66)
- Section headings: ~20-24pt bold, Water Cooler (#1C98E8)
- Body text: ~14-18pt, Ink (#0F2E66)
- Footer: ~9pt, muted gray (#94A3B8)

## Slide Layout Patterns

### Title Slide (Cover)
- Full gradient background: After Hours (#022043) at top → Tack (#6FC9D3) middle → Lunch Break (#FEC10B) at bottom
- Workday logo top-left (white text + orange arc)
- Title text in Ink (#0F2E66) at bottom-left, bold ~44pt
- Subtitle/date below in lighter tone

### Content Slides
- White background
- Left vertical gradient strip: ~30% width, Tack (cyan) at top fading to Lunch Break (gold) at bottom
- Bottom has a subtle warm gradient fade (white → Keyboard → Highlighter)
- "Workday Confidential" footer at bottom-left in gray
- Slide title: top-left in Ink, bold ~28pt
- Body text in Ink, bullets/sub-bullets

### Section Divider
- Left ~35% gradient strip (cyan→gold)
- Right side white with "OPTIONAL EYEBROW HEADLINE" in caps
- Large bold title in Ink
- "Workday Confidential" footer

### Bullets/Text Slide (Page 20)
- Plain white background (no gradient strip)
- Bold title top-left in Ink ~28pt
- Three levels of bullets: •, –, ▪
- "Workday Confidential" footer

### 3x2 Grid Slide (Page 11 - "What We Heard")
- White bg with subtle bottom warm fade
- Title in Ink bold at top-left
- 6 cards in 3×2 grid, headings in Water Cooler (#1C98E8) bold
- Body text in Ink, separated by thin lines
- "Workday Confidential" footer

### Numbered Steps Slide (Page 12)
- Left gradient strip, numbered 01/02/03 in Water Cooler
- Bold item title in Ink, description below
- "Workday Confidential" footer

### Two-Column Slide (Page 22)
- White bg, title + subhead top-left
- Two columns separated by thin blue divider
- Column headings in Water Cooler bold
- Body in Ink
- "Workday Confidential" footer

### Closing Slide
- Solid Ink (#0F2E66) background
- Centered Workday logo (white wordmark + orange arc)

## Color Combination Rules
- On dark backgrounds (After Hours, Ink, Ballpoint): Use Paper, Keyboard, Blue Sky for text
- On light backgrounds (Paper, Keyboard): Use Ink, After Hours, Ballpoint for text
- Headings on white: Water Cooler (#1C98E8) or Ink (#0F2E66)
- The vibrant secondary colors (Tack, Eraser, Smoothie, etc.) are for charts/data viz only
