## Adaptive Planning

Welcome to the Adaptive Planning book, where you can learn how to:

* Create budgets, forecasts, and what-if analysis using actuals data integrated from ERP and CRM systems.
* Model your business needs using dimensions, formulas, and links.
* Manage data with sheets, scenarios, processes, and workflow.
* Analyze data with reports, dashboards, and OfficeConnect.

To learn more about how to use our documentation, How to Use Workday Documentation on page 28 or Video: Workday Documentation Overview on page 33.

## Get Started

### Concept: Adaptive Planning

Adaptive Planning is a cloud-based planning and analysis solution that you can use for:

* Financial planning.
* Sales planning.
* Workforce planning.
* Additional capabilities.

#### Financial Planning

Use the financial planning capabilities to plan for revenue, cost, sales, and expenses by:

* Modeling your general ledger accounts.
* Creating budgets and forecast versions.
* Importing actuals data from ERP and CRM systems.

Also, integrate with Workday Financials to publish financial plans based on:

* The data and metadata of an Adaptive Planning plan version.
* A predefined Financials plan structure and plan template.

#### Sales Planning

Use the sales planning capabilities to help you reach your sales goals with:

* Territory Planning that enables you to assign sales representatives to enough territories for adequate coverage.
* Capacity and Quota Planning that lets you plan for staffing issues based on factors such as seasons.

#### Workforce Planning

Use the workforce planning capabilities to get an accurate picture of your workforce and adapt to changing requirements by:

* Creating effective hiring plans.
* Identifying gaps.
* Aligning with business goals.

#### Context
The Adaptive Planning model functions for:
*   Financial planning.
*   Sales planning.

Each data point in Adaptive Planning corresponds to an intersection of dimensions. All Adaptive Planning instances come with these dimensions:
*   Time: Months, quarters, and years from a calendar that runs from January to December.

You can also add custom dimensions and attributes to your model. Custom dimensions provide an additional coordinate to the intersections of your data. Attributes group the dimensions and the associated data. You can reference attribute groupings in reports and formulas.

Sales planning consists of 2 capabilities that work together:

## Capacity and Quota Planning

Capacity and quota planning helps you ensure that you have enough sales resources available to meet planned sales goals. Charts specific to sales planning help you test adjustments to achieve your staffing goals.

Capacity and quota planning typically includes planning for things like:
* Seasonality: The performance cycle of your sales team through a year.
* Ramp up: The process of bringing a new sales person up to full performance.
* Quota attainment: The degree of achieving planned sales goals.
* Productivity: The measurement of missing or surpassing sales quotas.

With capacity and quota planning, dashboards let you view your plan. From the dashboard, you can make adjustments to test scenarios for hiring and staffing.

## Territory Planning

Territory planning helps you create fair and balanced sales opportunities for your sales team. Use it to ensure that each territory is suitable for sales representatives. Is it big enough to meet the quotas of the sales representative? Is it small enough for the representative to cover it? You can also discover uncovered territory, which helps you avoid uncollected revenue.

Territory planning typically includes planning for things like:
* Altering sales head count.
* Adjusting sales quotas.
* Redistributing your sales representatives across geographies or industry types.
* Changing what geographical hierarchies make a territory.
* Accelerating new hire ramp-up so that new sales representatives gain responsibilities, faster.

## Sales Planning Versions

Versions in sales planning act more like scenarios. You can select different versions from the version prompt, which is visible from dashboards and sheets.

## Concept: Workforce Planning with HCM

Workforce planning helps you find the right people with the right skills for the right jobs. Workday Adaptive Planning provides different ways for you to plan for your workforce:

*   Personnel sheet in your financial planning instance.
*   A separate workforce planning instance with a foundational model to use with Workday Human Capital Management (HCM).
*   Other options you can discover with your customer success manager.

### Personnel Sheet

*   Modeled sheet that you can use to track and plan data for personnel.
*   Requires a financial planning instance.
*   Feeds into data in other sheets of your financial planning model.
*   Best suited more for the financial impact of personnel.

### Workforce Planning with HCM

*   Separate tenant from financial planning. You don't need to have a financial planning tenant to have a workforce planning tenant.
*   Requires HCM.
*   Requires that you configure Adaptive Planning for HCM.
*   Includes a pre-built foundational model with integration loaders and dimensions to sync with your HCM data and metadata.
*   Enables you to drill from Adaptive Planning sheets and reports to HCM data and objects.
*   Enables you to publish the data you collected from your plan to create positions and job requisitions in HCM.
*   Enables you to schedule integration tasks to continuously feed updated HCM data into Adaptive Planning.
*   Provides the **Manage Workforce Planning Configuration** task in Workday to automatically generate and load Adaptive Planning data, metadata, and modeled sheets.

### The Workforce Planning Foundation Model

The workforce planning foundational model comes with your separate workforce planning tenant. It includes:

*   Custom reports in Workday Customer Central: Holds all the Workday HCM data and metadata you need to populate the workforce planning model.
*   Custom dimensions: Match the Workday dimensions in the custom reports.
*   The Workday credential in Adaptive Planning **Design Integrations**: Connects Adaptive Planning to HCM.
*   Workday data sources and loaders in Adaptive Planning **Design Integrations**: Loads your HCM data and dimension values from the custom reports into the model.
*   Pre-built sheets: Configured to help you plan your workforce with the loaded data.

## Concept: Sheets

*Sheets* provide an interface to view, enter, and update data (such as actual ledger data, forecasts, and budgets). There are 3 types of sheets: standard, modeled, and cube.

Note: For documentation on building sheets and sheet settings, go to the Model Administration guide.

You can use sheets to enter data or change data. With sheets, you can:

*   Enter budget amounts for specific activities and fiscal periods.
*   Work with different departments to prepare detailed budgets.

Adaptive Planning stores the data on the server. When you save sheets, you're updating the stored data.

There are 3 types of sheets:

*   Standard: Simple spreadsheet with time across the columns and accounts or levels down the rows.
*   Modeled: Sheet with a list of records.
*   Cube: Multidimensional sheet that you can pivot.

## Concept: Rollup Cells in Sheets

Rollups sum or average the values of entries according to pre-set structures. Rollup cells represent the rollup value of time, accounts, dimensions, or levels. Not all rollup values are changeable in sheets. Look for blue cells to change rollup values.

Not all rollups are visible in sheets. You may have display option that hide rollups. Or, your admin may have hidden rollups to improve the performance of cube sheets. See Reference: Sheet Properties for Cube Sheets on page 1035.

### How Rollups Work

To change rollup values, you can change:

* The contributing cells and let the sheet calculate the rollup.

* The rollup and choose the breakback method to calculate the data in the contributing cells. For time rollups, you may not have to choose a method. The data you enter may automatically distribute according the method set up by your admin.

The breakback method determines how the value is distributed to contributing cells. For time rollups the breakback data populates to contributing cells, even if you can't see the contributing cells on the sheet,

©2026 Workday, Inc. All rights reserved

Workday Proprietary and Confidential

## Page 43

| Sheets | 43

unless the cells are locked for any reason. For level, dimension, and account rollups, breakback data populates only to contributing cells you can see on the sheet.

Rollups are based on their hierarchies in your model. Your administrator created the different hierarchies when they set up Adaptive Planning for your company.

• The time hierarchy is based on your calendar setup, most typically Month > Quarter > Year. Months contribute to the quarter rollups, which contribute to the year rollup.

• The level hierarchy is typically based on the organization of your company. Levels contribute to parent levels, which contribute to a single top-level, commonly called Total Company.

• The account hierarchies generally reflect your company's accounting books. There may be subtotals in standard sheets that add up values of accounts. Unlike account rollups, they don't follow the account hierarchy and they aren't changeable.

• Each custom dimension is a rollup. Dimension values contribute to the dimension rollup.

• Splits on standard sheets create rollups in the rows. These are data categories created on the sheet. They work like rollups, but they don't follow the structural hierarchies.

## Rollups Limitations by Sheet Type

For modeled sheets, you can enter values in time rollups only.

For standard sheets, you can enter values in time and account rollups.

For cube sheets, rollups of:

• Time and accounts display when they are in the columns, in the rows alone, or in the rows as the last nested dimension.

• Levels and custom dimensions only display in nested rows when they're the last nested dimension.

## Rollups in Columns

In modeled sheets with a time span and all standard sheets, time rollups appear in columns after the contributing cells (when visible). Example: The column for Quarter 1 appears after the columns for January, February, and March.

In cube sheets, accounts, levels, or any custom dimensions can be in the columns.

## Rollups in Rows

In all sheets, rollups in collapsed rows are gray (read-only). Expand the row to change contributing values or the rollup. When expanded, the rollup, labeled **Total**, appears as the last row.

• In standard sheets, accounts are generally the row headers and sheet data is displayed by account.

• When you view standard sheets by level, levels are the row headers and you can't change the value of level rollups.

• In cube sheets, you can drag time, accounts, levels and custom dimensions to the rows. These aren't always changeable.

• For cube sheets, your admin may have hidden total rows to improve the sheet's performance.

## Rollups as Filters

When dimensions are filters, any filter with an expand arrow is a rollup. When you choose rollups, you can view, but not change the data. When you choose a "leaf," or a filter that can't be expanded, you can change the data in the sheet. The values contribute to the rollup.

## Concept: Scenarios and Sheets

Scenario versions use base versions as starting points to build what-if scenarios or manage changes to the base version. You can change your base and scenario versions independent of each other and merge them later. You can use either data entry in sheets or Integrations imports to make proposed changes to base version data without affecting the base version. * Search cell notes.
* Search audit trails.
* Update Workflow statuses.

When you edit sheets for scenario versions, we disable your ability to:
* Add, edit, or remove sheet cell notes.
* Add sheet notes.
* Calculate formula values.
* Change your display options to set your sheet view as default for all users.
* Drill into NetSuite, Drill into Transactions, or Drill into Workday.
* Publish the plan.
* Show anomalies in standard sheets.
* Sync levels from sheets.
* Use consolidations functionality.

For standard sheets, you can compare scenario versions with baseline versions or other scenario versions.
